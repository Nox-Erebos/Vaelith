# Vaelith — Molecule Registry

> **Document status:** Working design document  
> **Scope:** Molecule identifiers, family compatibility, research lore, and their role in the specimen-solving system.

## Overview

The molecule name and ID are the same.

Molecules are grouped by compatibility with an attribute family.

## Molecule family summary

| Molecule family | Compatible attribute family | Available molecules |
|---|---|---|
| `MR` | Structural Systems | `MR-01`, `MR-02`, `MR-03` |
| `ED` | Energetic Systems | `ED-01`, `ED-02`, `ED-03` |
| `BC` | Reactive Systems | `BC-01`, `BC-02`, `BC-03` |
| `CR` | Coordinative Systems | `CR-01`, `CR-02`, `CR-03`, `CR-04`, `CR-05` |

## Research interpretation

The four molecule families represent broad areas of compatibility observed during Elarian experiments:

- **`MR` — Structural Systems:** molecules compatible with nullifying specimens exhibiting Structural Systems attributes.
- **`ED` — Energetic Systems:** molecules compatible with nullifying specimens exhibiting Energetic Systems attributes.
- **`BC` — Reactive Systems:** molecules compatible with nullifying specimens exhibiting Reactive Systems attributes.
- **`CR` — Coordinative Systems:** molecules compatible with nullifying specimens exhibiting Coordinative Systems attributes.

## Individual molecule registry

### Structural Systems — `MR`

**Family lore**

The `MR` group contains the first molecules developed for experiments involving the physical organization and integrity of Vaelith immune structures. Early laboratory observations established that members of this group could be used in experiments involving the nullification of Structural Systems specimens.

| Molecule ID | Research record |
|---|---|
| `MR-01` | An early synthesized compound retained for experiments involving Structural Systems specimens. |
| `MR-02` | A compound developed during subsequent Structural Systems research. |
| `MR-03` | A later compound retained in the Structural Systems research set. |

### Energetic Systems — `ED`

**Family lore**

The `ED` group was developed after researchers began recording unusual internal activity in Vaelith specimens. These compounds are used in experiments involving Energetic Systems properties. Their observed effects do not establish the source or nature of the energy being studied.

| Molecule ID | Research record |
|---|---|
| `ED-01` | A synthesized compound retained for experiments involving Energetic Systems specimens. |
| `ED-02` | A compound introduced during the investigation of recurring internal activity. |
| `ED-03` | A later addition to the Energetic Systems research set. |

### Reactive Systems — `BC`

**Family lore**

The `BC` group emerged from experiments involving specimens that undergo persistent internal changes or respond strongly to external conditions. Elarian researchers designate these compounds for experiments involving Reactive Systems properties.

| Molecule ID | Research record |
|---|---|
| `BC-01` | A synthesized compound used in experiments involving Reactive Systems specimens. |
| `BC-02` | A compound retained after experiments involving conditional changes in specimen activity. |
| `BC-03` | A later compound in the Reactive Systems group. |

### Coordinative Systems — `CR`

**Family lore**

The `CR` group was established for the rare and poorly understood properties associated with Coordinative Systems. It contains more available compounds than the other groups, reflecting the larger range of observed behaviors and the limited understanding of the final specimen.

| Molecule ID | Research record |
|---|---|
| `CR-01` | A compound retained for experiments involving Coordinative Systems specimens. |
| `CR-02` | A distinct compound in the Coordinative Systems research set. |
| `CR-03` | A compound introduced during later Coordinative Systems experiments. |
| `CR-04` | A compound retained after further investigation of the central specimen's unusual properties. |
| `CR-05` | The most recently added compound in the Coordinative Systems group. |
