# Open Questions and Unsettled Decisions

This file prevents proposals from being mistaken for established decisions.

## Research system

- What exact actions constitute an experiment?
- What feedback does an unsuccessful experiment provide?
- Are attribute-to-molecule mappings fixed across playthroughs, or randomized?
- How is Family IV made harder without requiring excessive blind trial and error?
- Can the player keep a research notebook or view previously confirmed discoveries?

## Molecular Fabricator

- Does synthesis consume energy, or does only fabrication/duplication consume energy?
- Does synthesis have a success probability, or is synthesis deterministic when valid?
- If synthesis can fail, what determines success? The latest upgrade direction does not include improving synthesis success.
- How are compound recipes represented in the interface?

## Bioassay Simulator

- What information does failure feedback reveal?
- Is testing asynchronous, allowing the player to work while a simulation runs, or does the player wait for the result?

## Progression and economy

- What is the money reward per case?
- What is the cost and maximum level of the shared machine upgrade?
- Is energy storage fixed, as currently assumed?

## Narrative

- What is Lucien's exact motive and justification?
- How much does Elara learn before the final reveal?
- What is Elara's response to the recording?
- Does the player get a final interaction after the reveal?
- Are the proposed Vaelith immune-cell names retained, replaced, or never shown?
