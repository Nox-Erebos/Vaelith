# Campaign Structure

## Overview

The campaign is fixed and finite. It contains exactly **10 cases** arranged across three phases and one final case.

| Section | Cases | New attribute families |
|---|---:|---|
| Phase I | 3 | Family I |
| Phase II | 3 | Family II |
| Phase III | 3 | Family III |
| Final | 1 | Family IV |
| **Total** | **10** | **4 families** |

The campaign becomes more complex as additional attribute families are introduced. There is no Easy / Medium / Hard profile selection.

## Case progression

- **Phase I — Cases 01–03:** Introduces the first family and the core research loop.
- **Phase II — Cases 04–06:** Introduces the second family and requires the player to account for a broader profile.
- **Phase III — Cases 07–09:** Introduces the third family and expands the possible combinations further.
- **Final — Case 10:** The central immune cell. Its profile includes three attributes from each of the first three families and one attribute from Family IV.

## Immune-cell identities

The following names were brainstormed as possible true Vaelith names. They are not confirmed canon. The player-facing case identifiers can remain simple labels such as `Case 01` through `Case 10`; the true names need not be shown to the player.

| Phase | Working true name | Player-facing identifier |
|---|---|---|
| I | Aelthir | Case 01 |
| I | Othryss | Case 02 |
| I | Saevryn | Case 03 |
| II | Khaelor | Case 04 |
| II | Vaerith | Case 05 |
| II | Ithraen | Case 06 |
| III | Xytheris | Case 07 |
| III | Oryndral | Case 08 |
| III | Thalvyr | Case 09 |
| Final | Aevoryth | Case 10 — Central Immune Cell |

These names are optional placeholders. The final case's central immune cell is the controller or central component of the Vaelith immune system. Its destruction effectively disables the species' ability to defend itself.

## Attribute profile rules

- Attributes have no numerical values. They are present or absent.
- The first three families each contain three attributes.
- Phase progression determines which families can appear.
- The final case contains exactly 10 attributes:
  - 3 from Family I;
  - 3 from Family II;
  - 3 from Family III;
  - 1 selected from the five possible Family IV attributes.
- Attribute combinations may vary, but the campaign structure and case count remain fixed.

The exact randomization constraints and whether the attribute-to-molecule mappings remain fixed between playthroughs are still open design decisions.
