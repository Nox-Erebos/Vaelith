# Characters

## Dr. Elara Voss — protagonist

**Role:** Player character; female researcher.

**Character direction:**
- Compassionate and devoted to bettering the lives of others.
- Genuinely believes the assigned work is medical research.
- Motivated by the possibility of helping people through scientific progress.
- Does not know that her compounds are being used to dismantle the Vaelith immune system.

## Dr. Lucien Veyr — commander

**Role:** The commander who commissions and oversees Elara's work.

**Character direction:**
- Controlled, articulate, authoritative, and manipulative.
- Uses calm professional language, even when discussing suffering.
- Treats the recording of the Vaelith woman as a means of taunting Elara.
- Reveals Elara's identity to the woman and makes clear that Elara developed the compounds.
- Should not rely on cartoonish villain behavior. His composure and casual cruelty are more unsettling.

## The Vaelith Woman — unnamed witness

**Role:** A member of the Vaelith species whose family and community have been devastated by the military operation.

**Character direction:**
- She has dignity and emotional depth.
- Her grief and anger are understandable responses to what has happened.
- She begins the recording pleading for mercy, then turns to anger and condemnation.
- She blames both Lucien and Elara for the deaths of her children and husband.
- She ends by cursing them and warning that they will not get away with what they have done.
