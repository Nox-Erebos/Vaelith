# Vaelith — Immune Cell Registry

> **Document status:** Working design document  
> **Scope:** The ten immune-cell specimens in the fixed campaign, including their dossier descriptions and narrative lore.

## Overview

The campaign follows ten specimens drawn from one ancient alien species: the Vaelith. The Elarian research division treats each specimen as a distinct research assignment.

The player encounters the specimens in a fixed sequence:

| Campaign phase | Specimens | Family introduced |
|---|---:|---|
| Phase I | `VY-01`–`VY-03` | Structural Systems |
| Phase II | `SY-01`–`SY-03` | Energetic Systems |
| Phase III | `NX-01`–`NX-03` | Reactive Systems |
| Final | `AE-01` | Coordinative Systems |

## Structural Systems

### `VY-01`

**Dossier description**

A stable internal structure exhibiting unusually consistent organization. Initial observations indicate that it plays a fundamental role in maintaining the integrity of the surrounding immune network.

**Research lore**

The first specimen documented by Elarian researchers. Its apparent simplicity initially led researchers to underestimate its importance. Continued examination reveals a degree of internal organization that is difficult to reconcile with the limited observations available.

### `VY-02`

**Dossier description**

An unusual structure containing alternating regions of differing internal density. Its behavior suggests a role in regulating interactions between neighboring immune components.

**Research lore**

Unlike `VY-01`, this specimen displays considerable variation between observed samples. Researchers have not determined whether these differences are incidental or represent deliberate adaptations within the Vaelith immune system.

### `VY-03`

**Dossier description**

An interconnected structure composed of elongated internal components. Despite repeated external disturbances, the structure demonstrates an unusual ability to preserve its overall integrity.

**Research lore**

The final specimen in the initial research phase. Its resilience raises questions about how the Vaelith immune system responds to sustained damage and whether structural integrity is maintained locally or across connected components.

## Energetic Systems

### `SY-01`

**Dossier description**

An anomalous structure exhibiting spontaneous internal energy displacement. Activity has been observed shifting between isolated regions without detectable external stimulation.

**Research lore**

The first specimen to challenge the Elarian researchers' existing understanding of Vaelith physiology. The source of its internal activity remains unidentified. Researchers cannot establish whether the observed energy is generated within the structure or transferred through an unknown process.

### `SY-02`

**Dossier description**

A structure exhibiting recurring fluctuations in internal activity. The observed fluctuations maintain a consistent pattern despite changes in surrounding conditions.

**Research lore**

Researchers have observed correlations between this specimen's activity and changes in other structures. The relationship is not yet understood, and no causal connection has been established.

### `SY-03`

**Dossier description**

An unusual structure in which internal energy progressively concentrates within specific regions. Prolonged activity appears to produce increasingly unstable conditions.

**Research lore**

This specimen displays the most pronounced energy concentration observed during the second phase. Its behavior suggests that energy distribution may be connected to the activity of other immune components, although the available evidence remains inconclusive.

## Reactive Systems

### `NX-01`

**Dossier description**

A highly reactive structure exhibiting continuous internal changes. Observations indicate that its composition rarely returns to its previous state following external interaction.

**Research lore**

The first specimen whose observed behavior appears fundamentally dependent on interaction with other substances. Researchers are uncertain whether these changes are defensive responses, ordinary operation, or a combination of both.

### `NX-02`

**Dossier description**

A seemingly inactive structure that exhibits sudden increases in activity when exposed to particular external conditions. Its activation mechanism remains unidentified.

**Research lore**

Its dormant state initially made the specimen difficult to study. Its sudden activity suggests that some Vaelith structures may remain inactive until specific environmental or internal conditions are met.

### `NX-03`

**Dossier description**

A structure demonstrating increasingly strong interactions between its internal components. Once established, these interactions become exceptionally difficult to disrupt.

**Research lore**

The final specimen before the central structure. Researchers increasingly suspect that the observed immune components are not independent defensive units, but parts of a coordinated system whose operation is not yet understood.

## Coordinative System

### `AE-01`

**Dossier description**

An unprecedented structure exhibiting activity across multiple connected immune systems. Preliminary observations suggest that it exerts considerable influence over the behavior of other Vaelith structures.

**Research lore**

Unlike the preceding specimens, `AE-01` appears to be more than an individual immune component. Its activity corresponds with changes observed throughout the wider Vaelith immune network. Researchers cannot establish whether it is a coordinating structure or the source of the system's organization.
