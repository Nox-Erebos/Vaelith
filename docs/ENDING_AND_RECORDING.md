# Ending and the Recording

## The reveal

After the final case, the player learns that they were not developing cures for a disease but rather creating biological weapons for the Elarion Empire.

Lucien, after thanking the player and revealing the truth, plays a recording of a conversation he had with a victim of the player's "cures". The recording shows Lucien conversing with a victim, followed by a reveal of what actually happened, a reveal of who was responsible, and the victim cursing both individuals.

The final dialogue is of Lucien ordering the execution of the player to "keep things confidential"; this is revealed to the player after the recording. The player shows some resistance, but in the end it is futile. Lucien again thanks the player for their cooperation and help and explains his twisted reason. After this the credits roll.
