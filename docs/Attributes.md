# Vaelith — Attribute Families and Attribute Registry

> **Document status:** Working design document  
> **Scope:** The four attribute families, their lore, and the individual attributes associated with each family.

## Overview

Elarian researchers have organized observed Vaelith immune properties into four broad families.

## Attribute Family Summary

| Family ID | Research Designation | Molecule Family Code | General Observed Behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ST` | Structural Systems | `MR` | Internal organization and structural integrity |
| `EN` | Energetic Systems | `ED` | Internal energy behavior and distribution |
| `RX` | Reactive Systems | `BC` | Internal transformations and external interactions |
| `CO` | Coordinative Systems | `CR` | Influence and coordination across connected structures |

## Family ST — Structural Systems

**Research overview**

The first family documented by Elarian researchers. Its observed properties concern the physical organization and internal integrity of Vaelith immune structures. These structures exhibit consistent arrangements, unusual internal patterns, and the ability to preserve their overall form under disturbance.

**Compatible molecule family:** `MR`

### `ST-01` — Ordered Internal Arrangement

**Description**

Exhibits a highly ordered internal arrangement in which individual components maintain consistent spatial relationships. Disruption causes the overall structure to lose its organization.

**Research lore**

This property is among the earliest recurring patterns documented in Vaelith specimens. Researchers have observed that structures exhibiting it maintain remarkably consistent internal arrangements, even when surrounding conditions change.

### `ST-02` — Variable-Density Regions

**Description**

Contains alternating regions of differing internal density. These regions appear to influence how the structure interacts with surrounding substances.

**Research lore**

The purpose of these regions remains uncertain. Their repeated appearance across observations suggests that they are not incidental irregularities, although their exact contribution to the immune system has not been established.

### `ST-03` — Interconnected Integrity

**Description**

Maintains overall structural integrity through interconnected internal components. Localized damage does not necessarily compromise the entire structure.

**Research lore**

Researchers have noted that damage to one region does not always cause the rest of the structure to fail. This has led to questions about how the Vaelith maintain continuity across their immune structures.

## Family EN — Energetic Systems

**Research overview**

The second family concerns unusual internal energy behavior. Observed properties include energy displacement, recurring fluctuations, and progressive concentration within particular regions.

The source of this activity is unknown. Elarian researchers have not established whether the energy is generated locally, transferred between structures, or produced through a process that current instruments cannot identify.

**Compatible molecule family:** `ED`

### `EN-01` — Internal Energy Displacement

**Description**

Exhibits spontaneous internal energy displacement between isolated regions, without detectable external stimulation.

**Research lore**

The observed displacement challenges existing Elarian assumptions about how energy behaves within the Vaelith system. Researchers have been unable to identify a corresponding external source.

### `EN-02` — Recurring Energy Fluctuation

**Description**

Displays recurring fluctuations in internal energy activity. The observed pattern remains relatively consistent under changing external conditions.

**Research lore**

The regularity of these fluctuations suggests that the activity is not entirely random. Some observations indicate correlations with other structures, but the relationship remains unexplained.

### `EN-03` — Progressive Energy Concentration

**Description**

Demonstrates progressive energy concentration within specific internal regions. Prolonged activity appears to produce increasingly unstable conditions.

**Research lore**

The concentration becomes more pronounced over time. Researchers have not determined whether this is a normal stage of operation or a response to changes elsewhere in the immune system.

## Family RX — Reactive Systems

**Research overview**

The third family concerns properties associated with internal transformations and interactions with external substances. Structures exhibiting these properties can undergo persistent changes, enter heightened activity under particular conditions, or develop increasingly resistant internal interactions.

These observations suggest that some Vaelith immune structures are highly responsive to their surroundings.

**Compatible molecule family:** `BC`

### `RX-01` — Persistent Internal Alteration

**Description**

Exhibits persistent internal changes following external interaction. The structure rarely returns to its previous state.

**Research lore**

The lasting nature of these changes distinguishes this property from temporary fluctuations observed in other specimens. Researchers have not established whether the changes are adaptive or simply an unavoidable consequence of interaction.

### `RX-02` — Conditional Activity Increase

**Description**

Remains relatively inactive until exposed to particular external conditions, after which internal activity increases substantially.

**Research lore**

This property complicates examination because the structure may appear inactive under ordinary observation. Researchers suspect that its activity depends on conditions that have not yet been fully identified.

### `RX-03` — Increasing Internal Association

**Description**

Demonstrates increasingly strong interactions between internal components. Once established, these interactions become exceptionally difficult to disrupt.

**Research lore**

The strength and persistence of these interactions suggest that the structure can maintain a stable internal arrangement through repeated disturbances. Its relationship with other immune structures remains under investigation.

## Family CO — Coordinative Systems

**Research overview**

The fourth family is the rarest and least understood. Its observed properties appear to influence the operation of multiple connected structures, sometimes at considerable distance from the source.

Unlike the other families, these properties cannot be adequately understood by examining a structure in isolation. Their effects suggest that the Vaelith immune system may have a central coordinating mechanism.

Only the final specimen, `AE-01`, is currently known to exhibit these properties.

**Compatible molecule family:** `CR`

### `CO-01` — Distributed Synchronization

**Description**

Appears to synchronize activity between distant structures according to a shared internal pattern.

**Research lore**

Researchers have observed similar activity patterns in structures that do not appear to be in direct contact. The mechanism responsible for this correspondence is unknown.

### `CO-02` — Distributed Activity Restriction

**Description**

Demonstrates the ability to restrict activity across multiple connected structures, with effects extending beyond its immediate location.

**Research lore**

The observed influence appears to extend through the wider system. Researchers have not determined how a single structure can produce changes in multiple distant components.

### `CO-03` — Process Alignment

**Description**

Causes independent internal processes to gradually align toward a common operational state.

**Research lore**

This property suggests that separate structures may be influenced toward coordinated behavior. Whether this alignment is continuous or occurs only under specific conditions remains unknown.

### `CO-04` — Persistent Operational State

**Description**

Allows established internal activity to persist despite disturbances affecting surrounding structures.

**Research lore**

Researchers have observed that certain processes continue even when nearby structures experience significant disruption. This persistence may help explain the resilience of the wider Vaelith immune system.

### `CO-05` — Remote Structural Influence

**Description**

Appears to influence the activity of other structures without requiring direct physical interaction.

**Research lore**

This is among the least understood properties observed in `AE-01`. Its apparent influence over other structures raises the possibility that the central specimen has a coordinating role within the wider immune system.
