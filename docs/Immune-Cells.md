# Immune Cells

## Overview

The campaign contains exactly **10 cases** arranged across three phases and one final case.

| Section | Cases | New attribute families |
|---|---:|---|
| Phase I | 3 | Family I |
| Phase II | 3 | Family II |
| Phase III | 3 | Family III |
| Final | 1 | Family IV |
| **Total** | **10** | **4 families** |

The Immune Cells get harder as the player progresses through each phase towards the final phase.

## Case progression

- **Phase I — Cases 01–03:** Introduces the first family and the core research loop.
- **Phase II — Cases 04–06:** Introduces the second family and requires the player to account for a broader profile.
- **Phase III — Cases 07–09:** Introduces the third family and expands the possible combinations further.
- **Final — Case 10:** The central immune cell. Its profile includes three attributes from each of the first three families and one attribute from Family IV.
