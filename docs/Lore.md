# Lore 

## The central premise

The player is **Dr. Elara Voss**, a female researcher devoted to improving and preserving lives. She accepts a research commission from **Dr. Lucien Veyr**, a senior commander who presents the work as an urgent medical initiative.

Elara is told that she is studying dangerous biological conditions and developing compounds that can neutralize them. She believes that successful treatments will save lives. Her intentions are sincere; she is not secretly malicious, and she does not knowingly participate in the military's true objective.

The truth is that the so-called diseases are components of the Vaelith immune system. The Vaelith are an ancient alien species, and their immune structures are defending their own kind. The military is using Elara's compounds to disable those defenses. Each successful treatment is, from the Vaelith perspective, another step in the destruction of their ability to protect themselves.

## The Vaelith

The Vaelith are ancient and mysterious creatures. These creatures possess a biological weapon that the Elarion Empire deems a threat to their survival.

## The military operation

The military's stated purpose is medical research. Its actual purpose is to exploit the compounds produced by Elara and use them against the Vaelith.

The deception should be believable within the game world:
- Elara receives a narrow research task rather than a full explanation of the operation.
- The systems present her work as treatment development.
- The military controls the information she receives.
- Lucien uses professional, controlled language rather than behaving like an exaggerated villain.
